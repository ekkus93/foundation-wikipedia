# STORE-002: Registered-media integrity audit (2026-10-26)

**Status:** Scoped read-only audit; STORE-002 remains unchecked.

The existing GC planners inspect currently unowned registered objects and unregistered published orphans. Neither verifies the integrity of **actively owned** media, which is the content a reader most needs to trust. Added `MediaRegistry::registered_media_candidates(max_objects)`, a deterministic, read-only SQLite inventory of all registered digests and sizes regardless of pack, cache or user-pin ownership. It validates digest syntax and the 64 MiB per-object cap, limits results to 1–10,000 entries, and rejects oversized result sets rather than silently returning an incomplete audit.

`media_gc::audit_registered_media_integrity(store, registry, max_objects)` walks that inventory and uses `MediaObjectStore::verify_object_size` to stream SHA-256 validation in bounded 8 KiB chunks. It checks declared sizes, reports verified object/byte totals and fails closed on missing, corrupt, unsafe or size-mismatched media. Unregistered published orphans are intentionally outside this audit and remain covered by `plan_unregistered_media_gc`; the two APIs must not be conflated.

**Safety boundary:** The registry query and filesystem reads are not an atomic cross-resource transaction. A concurrent installer or reader can change state during or after an audit. This API does not delete bytes, release attribution, certify publisher identity, prove a complete article media manifest or authorize garbage collection. Safe GC still requires reader/writer coordination, exclusive ownership revalidation at deletion, crash recovery and staging cleanup.

**Synthetic regressions:** Owned and unowned registered media both contribute to totals; an unregistered orphan is excluded and separately discovered; same-length corruption of an actively pack-owned figure fails; missing owned media fails; more than the configured bound fails even if every object is user-pinned; invalid limits fail. No media is removed by the audit.
